We’re comparing two lodging options for our group of 9–10 guests in Sunriver, Oregon. Two other groups already have houses reserved nearby: Cameron’s group at Todd #10 (17701 Todd Lane) and JR’s group at Todd #11 (17699 Todd Lane).

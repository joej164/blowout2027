Lodging
Options

Jan 14–18 · 4 Nights

We’re comparing three lodging arrangements for our group of 9–10 guests in Sunriver, Oregon. Two other groups already have houses reserved nearby: Cameron’s group at Todd #10 (17701 Todd Lane) and JR’s group at Todd #11 (17699 Todd Lane).

The Tradeoffs

  • Todd #1 (~$300/person) — Need a second portable bed, or someone takes an upper bunk. (Upper bunk would be an exception to our current no-upper-bunks plan.)
  • Sparks #5 (~$350/person) — Where we stayed last year. Farther from Cameron and JR (about a 4-minute walk to Todd #10).
  • Todd #1 + Todd #9 (over $400/person) — More comfort and privacy, but costs more. Someone else needs to book Todd #9.

Rough planning budgets; final costs depend on insurance, fees, and cabin occupancy.
